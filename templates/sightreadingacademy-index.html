Four terms, and the order is the method.

Each term assumes the one before it. That sequence is the whole difference between a student who keeps improving and one who plateaus at the point where most self taught players quietly give up.

01
Term one

Rhythm, out loud

Counting and subdivision until you can feel time without a metronome granting permission. Pitch can wait. Rhythm is the hardest thing to repair later, so it goes first.

02
Term two

Reading by shape

Intervals and contour instead of naming every note. You start reading a phrase the way you read a word, in one movement.

03
Term three

Technique on demand

Scales, stick control and bowing, introduced where the music needs them. Technique practised for its own sake gets dropped.

04
Term four

Other people

Playing with other people, holding time against a click, reading a page you have never seen. Everything before this was preparation.
